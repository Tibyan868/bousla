What is "Misinformation"? Have you encountered it before?

Misinformation refers to any inaccurate or false content that is published or shared—whether intentionally or unintentionally—that leads to misunderstanding among individuals or the public. It often spreads rapidly through media and social networks, especially during crises or conflicts, due to its emotional impact and ease of sharing without verification.
